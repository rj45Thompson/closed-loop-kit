# Tool Library: product requirements

## Purpose

Members of the neighbourhood tool library borrow tools instead of buying them. The desk volunteer records every loan.

## Loans

A member can borrow at most three tools at a time. A loan lasts seven days. A member with an overdue tool cannot borrow another until it is returned. The desk volunteer must scan the member card before lending.

## Returns

A returned tool is checked by the volunteer before it goes back on the shelf. A damaged tool is taken off the shelf until it is repaired.

## Fees

A tool returned late costs the member one credit for each day it is late. A member with no credits left cannot borrow.
